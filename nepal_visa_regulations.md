Multi-entry tourist visas extend to 5 years with a maximum 150-day cumulative stay per year, priced at $100 (1 year), $200 (3 years), and $300 (5 years). Visa fees remain $30 (15 days), $50 (30 days), and $125 (90 days) for single-entry, with additional $25 for VoA processing and $50 for multi-entry conversion. Digital payment integration is compulsory for all categories, and Nepalese embassies/consulates now validate VoA eligibility prior to travel. Failure to comply results in mandatory return flights or 5-day detention at TIA.

## 3. Online Pre-Arrival Registration & Digital Compliance for Expedition Teams

The Department of Immigration’s online pre-arrival registration portal (www.immigration.gov.np) is now the sole gateway for VoA applications. Teams must submit biometric data, passport scans, and itinerary details 72 hours before arrival. Failure to comply results in mandatory embassy visas or deportation. Digital payment integration is compulsory, with eSewa and Khalti as the primary payment gateways. For Kailash Mansarovar expedition teams, this means coordinating pre-registration with Tibet Travel Permit (TTP) issuance timelines, typically 14-21 days before departure. Teams should also account for a 5-day buffer period to resolve any discrepancies in digital submissions. The portal now includes a dedicated section for high-altitude expedition travelers, requiring additional documentation such as medical certificates and acclimatization plans.

- Online pre-registration via www.immigration.gov.np required 72 hours before arrival
- Biometric data, passport scans, and itinerary details mandatory for submission
- Digital payment via eSewa/Khalti compulsory; cash payments rejected post-January 2026
- 5-day buffer period recommended to resolve digital submission discrepancies

## 4. Transit Rules & Kathmandu Stopover Compliance for Kailash Mansarovar Teams

Transit passengers transiting through Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) must comply with the 24-hour transit rule, enforced with mandatory hotel bookings in Kathmandu. Failure to provide proof of onward tickets and hotel bookings within 24 hours results in 5-day detention or mandatory return flights. For Kailash Mansarovar expedition teams, this means pre-booking hotels for layovers, even if the transit is as short as 12 hours. The transit rule is strictly enforced, and teams should account for this in their itineraries to avoid penalties. Additionally, teams transiting through TIA should ensure their digital pre-registration is active for the entire duration of their stay, including any layovers.

- 24-hour transit rule strictly enforced at TIA
- Mandatory hotel bookings in Kathmandu for all transit passengers
- Failure to comply results in 5-day detention or return flights
- Digital pre-registration must cover entire transit duration

## 5. Embassy Validation & Pre-Approved Visa Structures for Non-Compliant Travelers

Travelers who fail to comply with the 72-hour pre-registration requirement or digital payment mandates must obtain visas from Nepalese embassies or consulates prior to travel. Embassy validation involves biometric data submission, passport scans, and itinerary details, with processing times ranging from 5-10 business days. For Kailash Mansarovar expedition teams, this means initiating embassy visa applications at least 3 weeks before departure to align with Tibet Travel Permit (TTP) issuance timelines. Embassy visas are priced identically to VoA fees but include an additional $50 service charge for expedited processing. Teams should also account for potential delays in embassy processing, particularly during peak expedition seasons (May-September).

- Embassy validation required for non-compliant travelers (failure to pre-register or digital payment)
- Processing times: 5-10 business days; initiate at least 3 weeks before departure
- Embassy visas priced identically to VoA fees + $50 service charge
- Peak season (May-September) may cause delays in embassy processing

## Frequently Asked Questions (Logistics & Permits)

**Q: What is the mandatory pre-registration timeline for Nepal Visa on Arrival in 2026?**

> A: The Department of Immigration requires a 72-hour advance submission via www.immigration.gov.np. Delays void VoA eligibility, necessitating pre-approved visas from Nepalese embassies or consulates.

**Q: Are cash payments accepted for Nepal Visa on Arrival in 2026?**

> A: No. Digital payment via eSewa, Khalti, or credit/debit cards is mandatory for all visa categories. Cash transactions are rejected post-January 2026.

**Q: What are the penalties for violating the 24-hour transit rule at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA)?**

> A: Violations result in 5-day detention at TIA or mandatory return flights. Compliance requires pre-booked Kathmandu hotels for the entire 24-hour transit duration.

**Q: How do I convert a single-entry Nepal visa to a multi-entry visa in 2026?**

> A: Conversion is not permitted on arrival. Travelers must exit Nepal and reapply for a multi-entry visa through a Nepalese embassy or consulate, subject to the 150-day annual stay limit.

**Q: What documentation is required for high-altitude expedition travelers in the pre-registration portal?**

> A: The portal now requires medical certificates, acclimatization plans, and passport scans. Additional documentation may be requested for Kailash Mansarovar expedition teams.
