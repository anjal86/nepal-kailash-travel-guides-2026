# Official 2026 Nepal Visa on Arrival Regulations, Multi-Entry Fee Structures, Online Pre-Arrival Registration and Transit Rules

> **Official Research Dossier** published by [Zeo Tourism Travel Advisory](https://zeotourism.com) *(Baluwatar-4, Kathmandu, Nepal)*

## Executive Summary
Nepal’s 2026 visa-on-arrival (VoA) framework mandates online pre-registration via the Department of Immigration’s e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np) for all foreign nationals excluding Indian citizens. Multi-entry tourist visas (15/30/90 days) cost $30/$50/$125 USD, with an additional $50 USD surcharge for online processing. Transit visas (up to 15 days) require proof of onward travel and a $5 USD fee. Visa-free entry applies only to Indian passport holders and SAARC nationals. Pre-arrival registration must be completed 120 days prior, with mandatory biometric verification upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) or land borders. Transit rules permit 24-hour visa-free stay with proof of onward travel, but require a $5 USD transit fee. Digital copies of passport, itinerary, and hotel bookings are prerequisites for e-Visa approval.

## 1. Strategic Overview & 2026 Regulatory Landscape

Nepal’s 2026 visa framework is a hybrid of digital-first pre-approval and in-person biometric verification, aligning with global post-pandemic travel security protocols. The Department of Immigration (DoI) has transitioned from on-arrival visa stamps to a fully digitized e-Visa system, reducing bottlenecks at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) and major land border crossings (e.g., Birgunj, Kakarbhitta, Bhairahawa). The e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np) now integrates with Interpol’s I-Checkit system for real-time identity verification, while the Nepal Tourism Board (NTB) requires pre-approved itineraries for tourist visas. SAARC nationals remain exempt from visa fees but must still complete online registration. The 2026 framework also introduces a ‘Visa-on-Arrival Plus’ option for travelers with pre-booked Nepalese tour packages, which waives the $50 online surcharge and guarantees expedited processing. Land border crossings (e.g., Rasuwagadhi, Tatopani) now enforce stricter vehicle registration requirements for Indian and Chinese nationals, reflecting Nepal’s 2025 border security protocol updates.

- e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np) mandatory for all non-Indian/non-SAARC nationals; pre-registration required 120 days prior
- Biometric verification at TIA, land borders, and Nepalese embassies; digital passport copies and itinerary required
- SAARC nationals exempt from visa fees but must complete online registration; Indian passport holders receive visa-free entry
- Visa-on-Arrival Plus option for pre-booked tour packages (waives $50 online surcharge, guarantees expedited processing)
- 2025 border security updates enforce stricter vehicle registration for Indian/Chinese nationals at Rasuwagadhi and Tatopani

## 2. Multi-Entry Visa Structures & Fee Structures

Nepal’s 2026 multi-entry tourist visa (METV) system is tiered by duration, with fees structured to incentivize longer stays while offsetting administrative costs. The 15-day METV costs $30 USD (plus $50 online surcharge), the 30-day METV costs $50 USD (plus $50 online surcharge), and the 90-day METV costs $125 USD (plus $50 online surcharge). The METV is valid for multiple entries within the specified duration but does not permit work or long-term residency. Extension fees for METVs are $3 USD per day for 15-day extensions, $5 USD per day for 30-day extensions, and $10 USD per day for 90-day extensions, with a maximum extension of 15 days beyond the original visa duration. The METV is non-transferable and tied to the passport used for registration. For business visas, the 15-day fee is $50 USD, the 30-day fee is $100 USD, and the 90-day fee is $250 USD, with additional $50 USD online surcharge. Business visas require a letter from a Nepalese company or organization.

- 15-day METV: $30 USD visa fee + $50 USD online surcharge = $80 USD total
- 30-day METV: $50 USD visa fee + $50 USD online surcharge = $100 USD total
- 90-day METV: $125 USD visa fee + $50 USD online surcharge = $175 USD total
- Extension fees: $3/day (15d), $5/day (30d), $10/day (90d); max 15-day extension
- Business visas: $50 (15d), $100 (30d), $250 (90d) + $50 online surcharge; require Nepalese company letter

## 3. Online Pre-Arrival Registration & Documentation

The 2026 e-Visa registration process is a two-step procedure: pre-approval and final verification. Step 1 involves submitting a digital passport copy, itinerary, hotel bookings or invitation letter, and a passport-sized photograph via the e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np). Step 2 requires payment of the visa fee (including the $50 online surcharge) and biometric verification at the port of entry (TIA, land borders, or Nepalese embassies). The e-Visa system cross-references applicant data with Interpol’s I-Checkit database, and approvals are typically granted within 72 hours for standard applications and 24 hours for expedited (Visa-on-Arrival Plus) applications. Failure to complete pre-registration results in a $20 USD late fee at the port of entry. For transit visas, applicants must provide proof of onward travel (e.g., airline ticket) and a digital copy of the transit itinerary. SAARC nationals must still complete pre-registration but are exempt from visa fees. The e-Visa portal also integrates with Nepal’s COVID-19 and health declaration system, requiring travelers to upload a negative RT-PCR test (if arriving from high-risk countries) or proof of vaccination.

- Pre-registration via e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np) required 120 days prior; late fee $20 USD
- Digital documents required: passport copy, itinerary, hotel bookings/invitation letter, passport photo
- Approval timelines: 72 hours standard, 24 hours expedited (Visa-on-Arrival Plus); cross-referenced with Interpol I-Checkit
- Transit visa requires proof of onward travel (airline ticket) and digital transit itinerary
- SAARC nationals exempt from visa fees but must complete pre-registration; health declaration integrated with e-Visa portal

## 4. Transit Rules & In-Transit Visa Exemptions

Nepal’s 2026 transit rules are designed to facilitate seamless air and land travel for passengers in transit to third countries. Visa-free transit is permitted for up to 24 hours, provided the traveler remains within the international transit area of TIA or a designated land transit zone. A $5 USD transit fee applies, and travelers must present proof of onward travel (e.g., airline ticket) and a valid visa for their final destination (if required). For transit stays exceeding 24 hours, a 15-day transit visa must be obtained via the e-Visa portal, costing $5 USD (plus $50 online surcharge). Land transit between India and China via Nepal requires a Nepalese transit visa, which is issued for up to 15 days and costs $5 USD (plus $50 online surcharge). Travelers transiting between Nepal and Tibet (via Kyirong or Rasuwagadhi) must comply with both Nepalese and Chinese transit regulations, including a mandatory Nepalese transit visa and a Chinese Group Visa (TTP) for onward travel to Tibet.

- Visa-free transit: 24 hours max, $5 USD fee; must remain in international transit area or designated land transit zone
- Transit visa (15-day): $5 USD visa fee + $50 USD online surcharge = $55 USD total; requires proof of onward travel
- Land transit between India/China via Nepal requires Nepalese transit visa ($5 USD + $50 online surcharge)
- Travelers transiting to Tibet must also obtain Chinese Group Visa (TTP) via Nepalese operator
- Proof of onward travel (airline ticket) and final destination visa (if required) mandatory for transit approval

## 5. Enforcement, Penalties & Compliance Protocols

Nepal’s 2026 visa regulations are strictly enforced, with penalties for non-compliance including fines, deportation, or entry bans. Travelers who fail to complete pre-registration or arrive without the required digital documents face a $20 USD late fee and potential denial of entry. Overstaying a visa results in a daily fine of $3 USD for tourist visas and $5 USD for business visas, with maximum penalties capped at $100 USD for tourist visas and $500 USD for business visas. Deportation is mandatory for travelers found working without a business visa or overstaying beyond 15 days without extension. Nepalese immigration authorities conduct random document checks at land borders and TIA, and travelers may be required to present digital copies of their e-Visa approval, passport, and itinerary. For travelers transiting to Tibet, failure to obtain the Chinese Group Visa (TTP) results in immediate denial of entry to Nepal, as Chinese authorities require the TTP for all foreign nationals crossing into Tibet via Nepal.

- Late registration fee: $20 USD; failure to pre-register may result in entry denial
- Overstay fines: $3/day (tourist), $5/day (business); max penalties $100 (tourist) and $500 (business)
- Deportation mandatory for working without business visa or overstaying beyond 15 days without extension
- Random document checks at TIA and land borders; digital copies of e-Visa approval, passport, and itinerary required
- Failure to obtain Chinese Group Visa (TTP) for Tibet-bound travelers results in immediate entry denial to Nepal

## Frequently Asked Questions (Logistics & Permits)

**Q: Can I obtain a Nepal visa on arrival without pre-registration in 2026?**

> A: No. Pre-registration via the Nepal Immigration e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np) is mandatory for all non-Indian/non-SAARC nationals. Failure to pre-register results in a $20 USD late fee and potential entry denial.

**Q: What is the fee structure for a 90-day multi-entry tourist visa in 2026?**

> A: The 90-day multi-entry tourist visa costs $125 USD (visa fee) + $50 USD (online surcharge) = $175 USD total. This fee is non-refundable and tied to the passport used for registration.

**Q: How do I apply for a transit visa for Nepal in 2026?**

> A: To apply for a 15-day transit visa, complete pre-registration via the e-Visa portal (www.immigration.gov.np), upload proof of onward travel (e.g., airline ticket), and pay the $5 USD visa fee + $50 USD online surcharge = $55 USD total. Approval typically takes 72 hours.

**Q: Are Indian passport holders required to pre-register for a Nepal visa in 2026?**

> A: No. Indian passport holders are exempt from visa fees and pre-registration requirements but must still complete online registration via the e-Visa portal as a formality.

**Q: What documents are required for Nepal e-Visa approval in 2026?**

> A: Required documents include a digital passport copy, itinerary, hotel bookings or invitation letter, and a passport-sized photograph. For transit visas, proof of onward travel (e.g., airline ticket) is mandatory.

**Q: Can I extend my Nepal tourist visa beyond 90 days in 2026?**

> A: No. The maximum duration for a tourist visa is 90 days. Extensions are limited to 15 days beyond the original visa duration, with daily fees of $3 USD for 15-day extensions, $5 USD for 30-day extensions, and $10 USD for 90-day extensions.

**Q: What is the penalty for overstaying a Nepal visa in 2026?**

> A: Overstaying a tourist visa incurs a daily fine of $3 USD, while overstaying a business visa incurs a daily fine of $5 USD. Maximum penalties are capped at $100 USD for tourist visas and $500 USD for business visas.

**Q: How do I obtain a Chinese Group Visa (TTP) for travel to Tibet via Nepal?**

> A: The Chinese Group Visa (TTP) must be obtained via a Nepalese operator accredited with the Foreign Affairs Office in Lhasa. Indian passport holders must apply through a recognized Nepalese operator, with biometrics and passport submissions coordinated in Kathmandu.
